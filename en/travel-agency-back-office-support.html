Outsourced back office support for travel agencies

BLER Travel Brazil's Back Office Agency Support is a tailored support, consulting and service offering for travel agencies, hotels and tourism companies that want higher productivity and lower operating and staffing costs.

How it works

We work as a virtual member of your team, handling tasks tailored to what you need. The service runs remotely and in real time; when needed, we also provide on-site assistance and production.

Behind it are more than 20 years of international experience in the travel industry and a partner office in the United States.

What we can do for your agency

  • Air reservations
  • Hotel reservations
  • Tour package building and production
  • Itinerary research and development
  • Support organizing corporate and leisure events
  • Support organizing incentive events
  • Personalized service for corporate and VIP clients
  • Training

Who it's for

Travel agencies

To absorb demand peaks, build complex proposals and itineraries, or serve VIP clients without adding permanent staff.

Hotels and tourism companies

To support reservations, events and corporate client service with an experienced team that already knows the market.
