Corporate travel management in Rio de Janeiro

BLER Travel Brazil is a Rio de Janeiro travel agency, founded in 2004, that manages business travel for companies in many industries — with personalized service and support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What we handle for your company

We take care of every stage of a business trip so your team can focus on its work. Our service adapts to each company's profile and needs, with the aim of cutting costs and solving problems quickly, without red tape.

  • Online booking and management systems
  • Air tickets
  • Hotel reservations
  • Travel health insurance
  • 24-hour emergency assistance
  • Corporate event organization
  • Car rental
  • Online reporting
  • Executive transfers
  • Incentive event organization
  • Private aircraft charter

Why companies work with BLER

More than 20 years in business

BLER was founded in 2004, and our team has more than 25 years of experience in the travel industry.

24/7 support, including abroad
